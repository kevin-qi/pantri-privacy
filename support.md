# Pantri Support

Hi, I'm the person who makes Pantri. If something isn't working, or you have a question or an idea, email me at **dev@justgoup.app**. I read every message.

It helps a lot if you include:

- What you were trying to do (for example, import a recipe from a link)
- Where it came from (a website, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, a photo, or pasted text), and the link if there is one
- What happened instead

You can also use **Settings → Send feedback** inside the app. If I ask for your Support ID, it's in Settings too, and tapping it copies it.

## Common questions

### A recipe didn't import

Pantri can import from recipe websites, YouTube, TikTok and Instagram links, photos, and pasted text. When an import fails, it's almost always because Pantri couldn't see the recipe itself. A few usual reasons:

- **The recipe isn't written down in the post.** A lot of videos only say the ingredients out loud or show them on screen, with nothing in the caption. Pantri reads the caption and description, so there's nothing for it to work with.
- **The post or page is private,** or needs you to be logged in to see it. Pantri can only read what anyone could see without an account.
- **The website blocks apps from reading it.** Some sites do this on purpose.

In all of these cases the fix is the same: copy the recipe text and import it as text instead (on the Import screen, choose **Text** and paste it). If the recipe is in the video comments or on a linked blog, paste that.

If the recipe came through but something looks wrong, open it, tap the menu, and choose **Reparse with AI** or **Edit recipe**. And please email me the link, because that's how I find and fix these.

### Importing from a photo

On the Import screen, choose **Photo** and take a picture or pick one (or a few) from your library. Cookbook pages, recipe cards and screenshots all work. Flat, well-lit photos where the text is easy to read give the best results.

The text is read on your phone. The photo itself is never uploaded anywhere.

### Sharing a recipe to Pantri

From Safari, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or most other apps, tap **Share** and pick **Pantri**. The recipe starts importing in the background. If you don't see Pantri in the share sheet, scroll to the end of the app row, tap **More**, and turn it on.

### Does it work offline?

Mostly, yes. Your recipes, baskets, lists and pantry are on your phone, so you can browse and check things off at the store without a signal. Importing a new recipe, and tidying a list with **Organize**, need an internet connection.

### Why are some things in my basket already checked?

Those are things Pantri thinks are already in your pantry, so you don't buy them twice. If you're actually out, just uncheck it.

### How do things get into my pantry?

When you check something off in a basket, it moves into your pantry a day later. You can also tap **Move to pantry** in a basket to do it right away, or add items yourself on the Pantry tab. If you'd rather manage the pantry by hand, turn off **Settings → Auto-stock pantry from checked items**.

### Where is my data, and how do I back it up?

Your recipes, baskets, lists and pantry are stored on your phone. There's no account, and nothing is synced between devices.

To keep a copy, go to **Settings → Backup & restore → Save backup**. That creates a file you can keep in Files, iCloud Drive, or anywhere else you like. To bring it back on a new phone, use **Restore** and pick that file. Restoring replaces what's currently in the app, so save a backup first if you want to keep both.

### How do I delete my data?

Go to **Settings → Delete all Pantri data**. This permanently deletes everything on our server tied to your copy of the app (the anonymous ID and the diagnostics linked to it), and clears all your recipes, lists and pantry on the phone. It can't be undone, so save a backup first if you might want your recipes later.

Deleting the app also removes everything stored on the phone. If you'd like me to delete server data for you instead, email **dev@justgoup.app**.

### Is my information private?

Pantri doesn't have ads and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. When you import something, the recipe text is sent to an AI service so it can pick out the ingredients and steps. The full details are in the [Privacy Policy](https://kevin-qi.github.io/pantri-privacy/).
