# Pantri — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** February 23, 2026

## Overview

Pantri is a grocery and pantry tracking app. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data Pantri handles and how.

## Data Storage

All your personal data — recipes, shopping lists, pantry inventory — is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain user accounts or store your data on our servers.

## External Services

When you import a recipe by URL, the following data is sent to third-party services for processing:

- **Recipe URLs and page content** are sent to OpenAI's API to parse recipe ingredients and instructions.
- **YouTube video metadata** may be fetched via the YouTube API when importing recipes from YouTube links.

These requests contain only the recipe content you choose to import. No personal information, device identifiers, or usage history is included in these requests.

## Data We Do Not Collect

- No personal information (name, email, location)
- No device identifiers or fingerprinting
- No analytics or usage tracking
- No advertising SDKs or ad-related data collection

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties for any purpose beyond the recipe import functionality described above.

## Children's Privacy

Pantri does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at **dev@justgoup.app**.
